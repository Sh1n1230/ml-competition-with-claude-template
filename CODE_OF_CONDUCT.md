# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.
Be constructive, assume good faith, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing,
and sharing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be
reviewed confidentially and handled fairly. Maintainers may remove comments, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe.
